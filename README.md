# Finance Tracker Application

Welcome to the **Finance Tracker Application**! This app allows you to manage and track your financial activities with ease. Whether you're tracking investments, earnings, or spending, this tool offers intuitive features with cutting-edge AI insights to help you understand your financial situation better.

**Deployed on:**
- Frontend: [Netlify](https://ftrack.netlify.app/)
- Backend: AWS Lambda, API Gateway

---

## Features

### 1. **Dark Mode & Light Mode**
   - Choose between dark or light themes for an enhanced user experience based on your preference.

### 2. **Sub Account Creation**
   - Create sub-accounts to track various categories or types of financial activities separately.

### 3. **Finance Analysis**
   - Analyze your finances in three dimensions:
     - **Investments**
     - **Earnings**
     - **Spending**
   - Get insights into your monthly and yearly financial performance, including paid commission and GST Tax calculations.

### 4. **AI Chatbot for Insights**
   - An AI-powered chatbot helps you by offering personalized financial insights, making complex data easier to understand.

### 5. **Password Protected with User Credentials**
   - Access your data securely with a password-protected login system.

### 6. **Dedicated Profile Page**
   - Each user has a dedicated profile page to view and manage their financial details.

### 7. **Inbuilt Tools**
   - **Calculator**: A simple yet powerful calculator to assist with financial calculations.
   - **Screenshot Tool**: Take quick screenshots for your records or notes.
   - **Notepad**: Write and store important notes directly within the app.
